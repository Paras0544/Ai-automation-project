# Well Priced: standing principles

Every reel, change and review is checked against this list. It comes from Paras's own feedback; add to it whenever he corrects something, so he never has to say it twice.

## Who it's for and what sells
- Indian guys ~18–28 on a budget. Promise: looks expensive, costs little.
- Single trendy items (jackets, sweatshirts, hoodies, shirts, cargos, sneakers…), never full outfits. Nobody buys a ₹10k outfit.
- Budget-friendly per item. Nothing that breaks the "well priced" promise (no ₹2.5k sunglasses).
- Variety: different styles and colours in one reel; never the same design in 4 colours; don't repeat a product type within ~3 days.

## Accuracy (never compromise)
- Every product, photo, name and price comes from the store's live data. Labels can't add or drop colours or invent features.
- Re-check price and stock right before posting; a reel with a changed price or sold-out item is retired, never posted.
- Captions list exact prices with a "prices checked <date>" note. Always disclose affiliate links (#affiliate).
- Amazon: no Amazon prices anywhere until they come from Amazon's own API (needs 10 sales/30 days). Tagged search links only.

## Money
- Only promote stores whose links pay (data/affiliate.json). Prefer the higher commission when products are equal.
- Keep Amazon Associates alive: 3 sales within 180 days of 28 Nov 2026.

## Reach and engagement
- What Instagram ranks: watch time, sends (shares) per reach, likes per reach, originality. Optimise for those, not vanity.
- Rotate formats (finds / guess the price / price drops, more as they prove out) and test hooks, hashtags, music, voiceover; let the numbers decide.
- Background music only if licensed for commercial social use; never copyrighted songs. Instagram's trending audio can't be added via the API.
- Account safety first: the account is new and was flagged once. Ramp posting slowly (1 → 2 → 3/day), no follow/unfollow tricks, no ads. Pause the ramp if flagged.

## Running the system
- Paras's time is the scarcest resource. Ask him only for things only he can do (logins, legal agreements, payouts) and batch them.
- Think ahead about consequences before he has to ask: if a change affects volume, catalogue depth, account safety, accuracy, compliance or cost, handle it in the same change.
- Every failure gets logged with its cause and fixed at the root; never silently drop work.
- Zero spend unless Paras approves it (he's open to a ₹500/month cap once the page earns).
